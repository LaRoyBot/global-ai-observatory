    Methodology & data honesty

    Synthetic demo data, not measured international token traffic. Every figure in this experience is fictional demonstration data embedded in the page.

    Geographic convention

    origin = the country where the inference service is being served. destination = the country associated with consumption of that service. This is a provisional modelling assumption for the demo — not a proven definition of AI imports or exports.

    Important distinction

    A route is a conceptual bilateral relationship, not a measurement of physical token exports or imports. AI tokens are not physical goods travelling across oceans. In reality, inference-serving location, customer location, billing location, model-development location and training-infrastructure location are all different concepts; this demo collapses them into one serving/consuming pair for illustration.

    Token directions

    • Input / prompt tokens (cyan) travel from the consuming country to the serving country — the reverse of the stored origin→destination pair.
    • Output / generated tokens (amber) travel from the serving country to the consuming country.

    Particle scale

    Particle size and density encode relative volume under a square-root scale. For each visible stream, weight = √volume. A global budget of at most 150 particles (75 in low-power mode) is distributed deterministically: every visible nonzero stream gets at least one particle where the budget allows, and the remainder is allocated proportionally to √weights using the largest-remainder method. Radius = clamp(1 + 2.2·√(volume / maxVolume), 1, 3.2) px. Particles do not represent individual tokens; exact numeric volumes are always shown in the summary, rankings, inspector and tooltips.

    Data source

    Embedded fictional demonstration dataset (FLOWS): six monthly snapshots, 2026-01 through 2026-06. Country boundaries: Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas 2.0.2 (Singapore is supplemented with a simplified Natural Earth 1:50m outline because 1:110m omits city-states). Rendering: D3 v7.9.0 orthographic projection on 2D canvas. No live feeds, no provider data, no analytics.

    Geographic clipping

    Routes and particles are computed along great circles and tested against the visible hemisphere every frame. Anything crossing behind the Earth is hidden immediately and restored when it returns — a circular canvas clip alone is not used.
