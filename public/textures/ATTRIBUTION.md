# Earth texture attribution

- `earth_atmos_2048.jpg` — NASA Blue Marble day map (equirectangular, with clouds).
  Source: NASA Visible Earth / three.js example textures. Public domain (NASA imagery).
- `earth_lights_2048.png` — NASA Black Marble city lights (2012 composite).
  Source: NASA Earth Observatory / three.js example textures. Public domain (NASA imagery).
- `earth_specular_2048.jpg` — ocean mask derived from the same NASA basemap.

These are basemap imagery only. They carry no data claims: the day/night
terminator is computed from the real current solar position; the token flows
rendered above them remain the labelled synthetic demonstration fixture.
